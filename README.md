# Vehicle Graphics Builder

Silver Fox Marketing's vehicle graphics on a 2020 Toyota RAV4, a 2018 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited and a 2024 Chevrolet Silverado. It covers windshield banners, body sides, hood graphics, Mini-Marks and ready-made seasonal themes, each shown at its printed size.

**Open it:** https://silver-fox-marketing.github.io/vehicle-graphics-builder/ on a computer, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install.

## How to use it

1. **Pick a vehicle** from the names across the top: RAV4, Wrangler or Silverado.
2. **Start from a theme.** The Themes tab opens first, with this month's sets at the top. One click puts a whole set on the vehicle, and Undo takes it back.
3. **Or add graphics one at a time.** Choose a spot (Windshield, Side windows, Rear window, Doors, Hood, Inside), or click a + marker on the vehicle, then click a card to put that graphic on. Click the card again to take it off. If it replaces something already there, the message says what and offers Undo.
4. **Turn and zoom.**
   - **Turn:** use the angle names along the bottom, the arrows, or the ← and → keys.
   - **Zoom:** scroll, pinch, or press + and −.
   - **Whole vehicle:** double-click or press 0.
5. **Review and get a quote.** The review sheet lists each graphic with where it goes and its printed size. From there:
   - **Get a quote:** opens an email to Silver Fox.
   - **Copy link:** copies the link to your build.
   - **Print or save as PDF:** prints the sheet.

**Tips**
- **Your build is saved in the page address:** share the link to show someone the same vehicle.
- **Hide graphics:** shows the bare vehicle, for a before and after.
- **Present full screen** (the screen button): shows the vehicle alone.
  - Keys 1–7 change the angle, T the theme, and V the vehicle.
  - Esc leaves.
